What's new

Admin, all in game Mount Admin and the Ledger

  • /mountadmin has four tabs: Players, Who rides what, Mounts and Shops. Everything saves at once and stays after a restart.
  • Give or take VIP, lock a whole mount or single colours to a player.
  • Set each mount Free, Item, VIP or item, VIP only or Off, plus one "Mounts need their item" switch.
  • Mounts tab: rename, turn on / off, set prices and ride numbers, add a new mount from a copy, no config edits.
  • Shops tab: place a mount shop where you stand; players walk up and press E.
  • The prop Ledger (/apadmin) and Mount Admin are linked: a player's page shows their mounts, with "Open in /mountadmin".
  • The old rklvipsystem /vipadmin is gone (it wiped perks it did not list).

Mounts

  • New prop mounts: the Gale Fan (a giant flying war fan) and the Dune Disc (a floating disc of sand). The broomstick, cloud and hoverboard were remade.
  • The land horses and the donkey were remade and renamed: Ironclad Destrier, Elven Palfrey, Wayfarer Courser, Peddler's Donkey.
  • The Ninja Tortoise swims, dives and surfaces in deep water and walks out in the shallows; on land SHIFT now runs on its legs.
  • The Ninja Kirin is the summoned Kirin itself (same model, same colours), rider on its saddle.
  • No height limit when flying. Wings come off when you land. No particles on mounts.
  • Smoother moves: gaits change without a snap, beasts back up and turn on the spot with their own steps, dragons keep their own wing beat, jumping beasts gather their legs, crouching riders sit right on heads and shells.
  • Fixed: a rider who sometimes went invisible to other players (different instance / dungeon buckets).
  • Removed from the mount list (nothing deleted, can be turned back on): the bear, armored wolf, donkeyboost, the ten tailed beasts, the Spider, the Ants and the Frost Wolf, and the ped copies of the Amethyst Wolf / Void Kitsune / Starforged Golem.

Pets and props

  • Riding your pet is one animal: press J on your summoned pet and its mount stands on its spot; get off and the pet is right there, following you. No smoke, no re-summon.
  • /petsummon also calls back a pet mount you left far away.
  • Every pet's mount is ridden through its pet (unless an admin sets "Normal mount too").
  • The pet follows better: it stays on the ground on jumps and flights, joins you on a High Jump, really stays on Stay.
  • Ten props are free for everyone (the One-Tailed Fox, three mount wings, two pets, two sigils, two pyres).
  • Shops and the Hall of Wonders show only what is for sale.

Getting a mount

1

Get it

Buy it at a mount shop (walk up and press E), use its item mount_…, or have it given by an admin. Some mounts are free for everyone. A mount's colours come with it, unless an admin gave you only some colours.

2

Call it

Press F6 (/mountvip) to open My Mounts: it lists every mount you can ride right now. Pick a colour, then Ride. You can also use the item. Send back in F6, or using the item again, puts it away.

3

VIP keeps it

A VIP player can press Lock in F6 to bind a mount from the bag to the character for good. A locked mount needs no item.

4

Your pet's mount

The ninja pets, dragons, gryphon, phoenix, wolf, kitsune and golem are ridden through their pet: summon the pet, then press J.

  • tap Shifttrot (on / off)
  • hold Shiftrun
  • Spacejump
  • Sback up
  • Hroar (dragons, beasts)

In the air

  • A / Dturn

Animated props and pets

1

Wear props

/animatedprop opens your Regalia: tails, wings, horns, pets and more. Equip, pick a colour, take it off. Using a prop item equips it; it stays safe in your Regalia while worn.

2

Your pet

/petmenu shows your pet's moves, Stay, Roam and Stop. /petsummon calls your pet out at full size or sends it back to your shoulder.

3

Ride your pet

Summon it, press J: its mount stands where the pet stood. Get off and your pet is right there again, following you. Left its mount far away? /petsummon calls it back.

4

Prop moves

/apflare, /apswish, /apshake, /apflick play a move on the prop you wear, when it has that move. Everyone near you sees it.

For admins ADMIN

Players

Search a player online by name or #id. Give or take VIP Stable; for every mount, Lock to them (every colour) or Take back; Colours opens chips to give single colours. "In their bag" and "Locked to them" show what they already have.

Who rides what

Each mount gets a mode (below). One switch for "Mounts need their item". A pet's mount has Normal mount too: on = it can also be ridden without its pet. Search, then "Set every mount shown to…" changes many at once.

Mounts

Every mount on one list: On / Off, its name, shop price, and its ride numbers (speed, boost, climb, walk / run, top speed, stamina, jump). Back to config undoes a row. Add a mount makes a copy under a new name with an existing model.

Shops

Add a shop where you stand and name it. Per shop: On / Off, Move here, Go there, Delete (click twice), the stable hand ped, the map icon, and what it sells (every mount on sale, or chosen mounts). Players press E there.

Freeeveryone rides it
Itemneeds its item (default)
VIP or itemVIP without the item, others with it
VIP onlythe item alone is not enough
Offnobody; the item is kept

A mount locked to a player always works for them, unless it is Off. Every change is logged in the server console and the player is told.

+

Props: /apadmin

The Steward's Ledger: give a prop (as an item or as access, for a time), activate a pet's mount, Holders (who has what, offline too), VIP tiers, permissions, shops, the Showroom and the log. A player's page shows their mounts with "Open in /mountadmin". /apfit opens the Fitting Bench.

+

Tuning: /mountdev

The full live editor for a mount (speeds, jump height, seats, camera), saved for every player. /mountfit measures a mount's height on the ground again.

Who counts as an admin: for mounts, the ACE rklmounts.admin or the groups admin, superadmin, god, owner. For props, the roles in the Ledger's Permissions tab.

Server setup

Everything starts with ensure [rkl]. Keep these folders together:

resources/[rkl]/[mounts models]/rklmounts            the mount script (land + prop mounts inside)
resources/[rkl]/[mounts models]/rklflyingmounts      dragons, gryphon, phoenix, wings
resources/[rkl]/[mounts models]/rklmountninja        the ninja pet mounts
resources/[rkl]/[mounts models]/rklmountfantasyland  wolf, kitsune, golem
rklpet_ninjakirin  (with the pet packs)              the Kirin: pet, summon and mount
resources/[rkl]/[animatedprops]/rklanimatedprop      props + pets, with its addon packs
  • If a model pack is missing, the server says so at start and those mounts cannot be called.
  • Do not run the old rklmountdragons, rklmountfantasyfly or rklmountwings next to rklflyingmounts: the same models twice.
  • What admins set in game lives in rklmounts/data/: mounts.json, mount_rules.json, shops.json, mount_overrides.json. Back these up with the server.
  • VIP and mount locks are in the database table awaken_vip_perks (oxmysql).
  • After an update, restart the changed resources (or the server).
