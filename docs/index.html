IWhat's new

Admin, all in game

  • /mountadmin: five tabs, Players, Who rides what, Mounts, Shops and Holders. Saved at once, kept after a restart.
  • Pick a player and give them exactly the mounts (or single colours) they may ride.
  • Assigned only new: a mount only for the players it was given to. VIP alone is not enough.
  • Holders new: everyone who has access, offline too. Take it back from there, even from a player who is not online.
  • Rename mounts, turn them on or off, set prices and speeds, add a new mount from a copy.
  • Place mount shops where you stand; players walk up and press E.

Mounts

  • New: the Gale Fan, a giant flying war fan, and the Dune Disc, a floating disc of sand. The broomstick, cloud and hoverboard were remade.
  • The horses and the donkey remade: Ironclad Destrier, Elven Palfrey, Wayfarer Courser, Peddler's Donkey.
  • The Ninja Tortoise swims, dives and runs on its legs; the Ninja Kirin is the summoned Kirin itself.
  • No height limit in the sky. Wings come off when you land.
  • Smoother moves everywhere; a rider no longer goes invisible to others.

Pets & props

  • Your pet and its mount are one animal: J on your summoned pet, and when you get off it is right there.
  • /petsummon calls back a pet mount left far away.
  • The pet follows better on jumps, flights and Stay.
  • Ten props are free for everyone. Shops show only what is for sale.

IIGetting a mount

1

Earn it

Buy it at a mount shop (walk up, press E), use its item, or receive it from an admin. Some mounts are free for all. Its colours come with it, unless you were given only some.

2

Call it

Press F6 (/mountvip) for My Mounts: every mount you may ride right now. Pick a colour, then Ride. Send back puts it away.

3

Keep it

A VIP can press Lock in F6 to bind a mount from the bag to the character for good.

4

Your pet's mount

The ninja pets, dragons, gryphon, phoenix, wolf, kitsune and golem are ridden through their pet: summon it, then press J.

VProps & pets

1

Wear props

/animatedprop opens your Regalia: tails, wings, horns, pets and more. Equip, pick a colour, take it off. A worn prop is kept safe while you wear it.

2

Your pet

/petmenu: its moves, Stay, Roam, Stop. /petsummon calls it out at full size or sends it back to your shoulder.

3

Ride your pet

Summon it and press J: its mount stands where it stood. Dismount and your pet is there again, following you.

4

Prop moves

/apflare, /apswish, /apshake, /apflick play a move on the prop you wear, for all to see.

VIFor admins

Players

Find a player online. Give or take VIP. For each mount: Lock to them (every colour), Take back, or Colours to give single colours. This is how you choose exactly what each player may ride.

Who rides what

Each mount gets one of the modes below, plus one switch for "Mounts need their item". A pet's mount can also be a Normal mount too. Search, then set every mount shown at once.

Mounts

Every mount on one list: on or off, its name, price and riding numbers. Back to config undoes a row; Add a mount copies one under a new name.

Shops

Add a shop where you stand. Move it, visit it, give it a stable hand and a map mark, and choose what it sells. Players press E there.

Holders new

Everyone who holds VIP, a mount, a single colour or a pet's mount, online or offline. Search a name, #id or mount; filter by kind or by Online / Offline. The ✕ on a row takes it back (click twice), Take back all clears a player.

To give a player only certain mounts: set those mounts to Assigned only, then in Players lock to each player what they may ride. A mount locked to a player always works for them, unless it is Off.

Props: /apadmin

The Steward's Ledger: give a prop (as an item or as access, for a time), turn on a pet's mount, see who holds what, VIP tiers, roles, shops, the Showroom and the log. /apfit opens the Fitting Bench.

Tuning

/mountdev edits a mount live (speeds, jump, seats, camera) for everyone. /mountfit measures a mount's height on the ground again.
