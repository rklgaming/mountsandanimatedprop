IWhat's new

IIGetting a mount

1

Earn it

Buy it at a mount shop (walk up, press E), use its item, or receive it from an admin. Some mounts are free for all. Its colours come with it, unless you were given only some.

2

Call it

Press F6 (/mountvip) for My Mounts: every mount you may ride right now. Pick a colour, then Ride. It comes to you; press E to get on. Send back puts it away.

3

Keep it

A VIP can press Lock in F6 to bind a mount from the bag to the character for good.

4

Your pet's mount

The ninja pets, dragons, gryphon, phoenix, wolf, kitsune and golem, the Necrotic Bear, Six-Eyed Spider, King Cobra, Stormcrest Hawk and Ninja Kirin are ridden through their pet: summon it, then press J.

IIIRiding keys

Pick where you ride; the keys that work there light up. Click a key to see what it does.

Every key can be changed: Settings › Key Bindings › FiveM.

IVEvery model

Every mount and prop we sell, as it looks in game. Free ones come with the script; the others are add-on packs you drop next to it. Point at one to see it turn, click it to see it big. New shows the latest.

Also free with Dynamic Mounts: GTA's own animals, set up to ride: dolphin, orca, manta, hammerhead, tiger shark, war boar, ox, stag, cougar and panther.

VProps & pets

1

Wear props

/animatedprop opens your Regalia: tails, wings, magic circles, auras, pets and more. Equip, pick a colour, take it off. A worn prop is kept safe while you wear it.

2

Your pet

/petmenu: its moves, Stay, Roam, Stop. /petsummon calls it out at full size or sends it back to your shoulder.

3

Ride your pet

Summon it and press J: its mount stands where it stood. Dismount and your pet is there again, following you.

4

Prop moves

/apflare, /apswish, /apshake, /apflick play a move on the prop you wear, for all to see.

VIFor admins

/mountadmin has six tabs. Take the tour: pick a tab, then a numbered part.

Who can ride it?

Pick a mode (and the "Mounts need their item" switch) to see who may ride a mount set to it.

To give a player only certain mounts: set those mounts to Assigned only, then in Players lock to each player what they may ride. A mount locked to a player always works for them, unless it is Off.

Props and pets: /apadmin

The Steward's Ledger: give a prop (as an item or as access, for a time), turn on a pet's mount, see who holds what, VIP tiers, roles, shops, the Showroom and the log. /apfit opens the Fitting Bench.

Tuning

/mountdev edits a mount live for everyone. A speed, top speed, stamina or jump set there goes to every colour of that mount. /mountfit measures a mount's height on the ground again.
